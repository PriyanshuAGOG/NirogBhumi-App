How to raise a grievance

  1. Email the Grievance Officer from the contact registered to your account, describing your request or concern.
  2. Include enough detail to verify your identity (the phone/email you signed up with).
  3. We acknowledge and work to resolve it within the legally required timeframe.

Escalation

If your grievance is not resolved to your satisfaction, you may lodge a complaint with the Data Protection Board of India, the authority established under the DPDP Act, 2023.
