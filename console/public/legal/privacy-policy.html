We collect data you enter, data you import from Health Connect, and technical data generated as you use the app. We do not require precise GPS location. We do not sell your personal or health data, and we do not use it for third-party advertising.

2. Why we process it (purposes)

  • To provide the core service: track your readings, generate reports and insights, and run your Care+ program.
  • To connect you with an assigned coach/expert when you book care or join a program (only then).
  • To send reminders and service notifications you have enabled.
  • To process orders and consultations you request.
  • To keep the service secure, prevent fraud and abuse, and meet legal obligations.
  • Optional, separate consent: de-identified aggregate research to improve the program; product-update/marketing messages. Both are off by default and independently withdrawable.

We process your data only for these specified purposes and limit collection to what each purpose needs.

3. Consent & its withdrawal

We process your personal data on the basis of your consent (and, where applicable, for certain legitimate uses permitted by law). At sign-up we give you a clear notice and ask for your consent to the required processing (health-data storage and the medical disclaimer) and separately for optional purposes. Each consent is recorded with the date, the policy version, and the specific purposes.

You can withdraw consent at any time — as easily as you gave it — in Profile → Privacy & consent for optional consents, or by anonymizing/deleting your account for the required processing (after which the app can no longer function). Withdrawing consent does not affect processing already carried out lawfully.

4. Who we share it with

  • Your assigned coach/expert — sees your relevant logs only when you book care or join their program, and only for members in their own batch.
  • Data Processors acting on our instructions under contract: cloud hosting and database (Google Firebase / Google Cloud), crash & analytics diagnostics, push notifications, and a payment gateway for transactions. They may process data outside India subject to appropriate safeguards.
  • Authorities where required by law.

Your health records are not public and are never shared with other members without your action.

5. Retention

We keep your personal data while your account is active and for as long as needed for the purposes above. When a purpose is served, or you delete your account, we delete or irreversibly anonymize your data — except limited records we must keep for legal, tax, payment, fraud-prevention or dispute-resolution reasons. See the Account & data deletion page for details and timeframes.

6. Your rights under the DPDP Act

  • Access — a summary of the personal data we process about you.
  • Correction & completion — fix inaccurate or incomplete data.
  • Erasure — delete your data (subject to lawful retention).
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, for optional processing.
  • Grievance redressal — a readily available means to raise concerns (see §9).
  • Nominate — nominate another individual to exercise your rights in the event of death or incapacity.

Exercise these in the app (Profile → Privacy & consent, and Data Controls) or by contacting our Grievance Officer. If you remain unsatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

7. Children & dependents

The app is intended for adults (18+). You may create a dependent/family profile to help manage a family member's health. If that person is under 18 (or a person with a lawful guardian), you confirm you are their parent or lawful guardian and consent on their behalf. We do not knowingly use a child's data for tracking, behavioural monitoring, or targeted advertising, consistent with the DPDP Act. If you believe a child's data was provided without proper consent, contact our Grievance Officer and we will delete it.

8. Security & breach notification

We protect your data with encryption in transit, access-controlled storage, role-based access (coaches see only their own batch), device attestation (Play Integrity / App Check), and audited administrative access. No system is perfectly secure. If a personal-data breach occurs, we will notify the Data Protection Board and affected users as required by the DPDP Act.
