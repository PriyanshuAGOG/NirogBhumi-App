Delete your Nirog Bhumi account & data

Applies to the Nirog Bhumi app (package in.nirogbhumi.app). Last updated: [date — set at publish].

You can permanently delete your Nirog Bhumi account and the personal data associated with it. You do not need to keep the app installed to make this request.

Option 1 — In the app (fastest)

  1. Open Profile → Privacy & consent → Manage my data (Data Controls).
  2. Tap Delete / anonymize my account and confirm.
  3. Your access is disabled while the request is verified and processed. You receive confirmation when it completes.

Option 2 — By email (no app needed)

Send a deletion request from the email address registered to your account to:

Email: privacy@nirogbhumi.com [confirm/create this mailbox]

Include the phone number or email you signed up with so we can verify it is you. We respond within the timeframe required by applicable law and, on completion, confirm to your verified contact.

What is deleted

On a verified deletion request we permanently delete or irreversibly anonymize the personal data linked to your account, including:

  • Your identity and contact details (name, phone, email, city).
  • Your health logs — glucose, blood pressure, sleep, walking/activity, weight, medications, daily check-ins.
  • Uploaded lab reports and files, connected-device import data, consultations, program activity, and orders.
  • Account profile, family/dependent profiles you created, and notification records.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023 and other applicable law you may request access, correction, and erasure of your personal data, and withdraw consent. See our Privacy Policy and Grievance & complaints page. If unsatisfied, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.
